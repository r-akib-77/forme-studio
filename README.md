## 👕 Overview

**FORME STUDIO** is a fashion storefront concept focused on versatile wardrobe essentials: heavyweight T-shirts, oversized fits, jerseys, shirts, bottoms, and accessories.

The experience is built around a simple retail idea: fewer, better pieces that work together. The storefront combines editorial campaign imagery, category-led discovery, product cards, featured collections, bestsellers, brand storytelling, and supporting shopping information in one cohesive interface.

Designed around a fictional brand based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, the project demonstrates how a contemporary apparel label can present its identity and product range through a polished online storefront.

**Live website:** https://forme-studio.pages.dev/

## 🧭 Brand Direction

FORME STUDIO is built around the concept of a deliberate wardrobe: reliable fits, versatile colours, and everyday pieces that can be combined without overcomplicating personal style.

The brand presentation emphasizes three ideas:

- **Fit first** — silhouettes and garment proportions take priority over short-lived trends.
- **Less noise, better choices** — a focused range makes product discovery easier.
- **Everyday versatility** — core pieces are presented as items that can work across different routines and settings.

This direction informs the visual language, product categories, editorial content, and shopping journey.

## ✨ Features

### 1. Editorial hero section
A campaign-led introduction establishes the brand's visual identity and directs visitors toward new arrivals and collections.

### 2. Category-based discovery
Dedicated navigation and category cards organize the catalogue into recognizable product groups, helping shoppers move quickly toward relevant items.

### 3. Featured product cards
Product cards display sample product names, pricing, promotional labels, colour options, and merchandising badges.

### 4. New arrivals and bestsellers
Dedicated product sections create multiple discovery paths for visitors who want to browse recent additions or featured styles.

### 5. Collection storytelling
Editorial content explains the brand's approach to fit, fabric, wardrobe versatility, and considered product selection.

### 6. Campaign imagery
Fashion photography and visual campaign sections give the storefront an editorial character rather than presenting it as a plain product grid.

### 7. Shopping information
The layout includes destinations for delivery information, exchange and returns, size guidance, contact information, and legal pages.

### 8. Newsletter interaction demo
A newsletter-style form demonstrates the interface for collecting email interest. The live demo indicates that validation happens in the browser and addresses are not transmitted.

### 9. Social navigation
Social media links are included in the storefront layout. The current demo uses placeholder destinations, so they should be connected to real brand profiles before production use.

### 10. Responsive-oriented layout
The content is structured for browsing across common screen sizes. Confirm actual behavior on mobile, tablet, and desktop before describing responsive support as fully tested.

## 🛒 Shopping Experience

The storefront is organized around a straightforward discovery journey:

1. **Discover the brand** — enter through the editorial hero and explore the featured collection.
2. **Choose a category** — navigate to T-shirts, oversized fits, jerseys, shirts, bottoms, or accessories.
3. **Browse products** — compare product imagery, names, sample prices, and available colour options.
4. **Explore featured edits** — use new arrivals, bestsellers, and campaign content to discover more styles.
5. **Find supporting information** — visit the size guide, delivery information, exchange policy, or contact section as needed.

This describes the intended storefront experience, not a completed production checkout workflow.

## 🎨 Design Principles

The interface is built around a fashion-editorial approach: product imagery establishes mood, while the navigation and content structure support product discovery.

- **Editorial visual hierarchy:** campaign imagery and strong headlines establish the brand before the catalogue.
- **Product-first presentation:** product cards focus attention on imagery, naming, pricing, and options.
- **Consistent category navigation:** shoppers can move between product groups without learning a new interface.
- **Focused brand language:** concise copy reinforces the idea of versatile, considered essentials.
- **Clear shopping support:** size, delivery, and exchange information have dedicated destinations.
- **Restrained interaction design:** transitions and interactive details should support usability rather than distract from products.
- **Performance awareness:** properly sized images and efficient assets help preserve a smooth browsing experience.

## ⚠️ Demo Scope and Limitations

FORME STUDIO is a **frontend demonstration**, not a live online shop.

- The brand is fictional and created for this design demonstration.
- Products, prices, stock labels, discounts, and reviews are sample content.
- Testimonials are illustrative and are not verified customer reviews.
- The website does not process live payments or real orders.
- The newsletter interaction validates the address in the browser; it does not transmit or subscribe the address.
- Social links use placeholder destinations.
- Delivery, exchange, and product information should not be treated as real commercial policies.

Before adapting this design for a real business, replace sample content, verify all claims, connect real support destinations, and implement the required commerce services securely.
